{{ partial "section-head.html" (dict "num" "03" "label" "Why “Sidechain”") }}

A side chain is the part of an amino acid that makes it different from the other nineteen. The models are named the same way: each series is an amino acid whose side chain matches the model's character, and a number counts entries within it. A letter suffix marks a one-knob variant — SER-1p is SER-1 with Poisson cells, SER-1n is SER-1 without shrinkage.
