How the numbers were made. Control cells only, {{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $n }} per context. Counts were scaled to 10,000 per cell and log-transformed; each context's map uses its 2,000 most variable genes (scanpy, Seurat flavour), scaled, a 50-component PCA, a 15-neighbour graph on the first 30 components, UMAP and Leiden clusters (resolution 0.5). The joint map takes the 2,000 genes variable in the most contexts. The guide checks compare each guide's centre with the context's in the first 20 components against 500 shuffles of the labels. The assay is 10x Flex (probe-based) read on an Ultima UG100, per Arc; reads and sequencing saturation are not in the released files, and the Flex panel has no ribosomal-protein genes.

Data: the control cells Arc Institute released for the Virtual Cell Challenge 2026, computed {{ $idx.stage_built }}. Every chart downloads as SVG from its toolbar.
